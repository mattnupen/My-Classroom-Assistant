# Class Tools: what each app is for

**Class Tools** is the page you double-click to open your offline apps: open the `local-tools` folder in your classroom assistant folder and double-click `ClassAI-dashboard`. It opens in your web browser. (Can't find it? Search for ClassAI-dashboard in Finder on a Mac or File Explorer on Windows.) Every app is in its left sidebar. The apps run on your computer, and nothing you open in them is sent anywhere.

It's not the same as **your class page**, the summary your AI keeps and shows you in the chat.

| App | What it's for | When | What reaches the chat |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Class Tools** (sidebar, top) | The home page: what each app does and how it fits with your chat | Your first visit | Nothing |
| **Class Pulse** | Turns your gradebook into a weekly class summary with no names | Once a week, if your challenge is missing work and you'd rather not count by hand | The summary, pasted in (or saved to your pulses folder) |
| **Progress Cards** | Prints each student a card of what they still owe | Monday, or before a catch-up day | Nothing |
| **Parent Messages** | One template, a personal note home for every student | Before conferences or report cards | Nothing (your AI can write the templates) |
| **Gradebook Analytics** | Every student's missing work and grade in one sortable table | When you want the full picture | Nothing |
| **Feedback Cleaner** | Takes names out of student survey answers and sums them up | After a class survey | The cleaned answers, if you paste them in |
| **Badges** | Prints certificates for effort and comebacks | Celebration days, end of a unit | Nothing |
| **Random Groups** | Fair groups that keep chosen students apart | Before group work | Nothing |
| **App Studio** | Ideas for new apps your AI can build for you | When you wish a tool existed | A short request you copy in, describing the tool (no student info) |
| **Demo Semester** | A made-up class through a whole term | Before you start | Nothing |

Every app that reads a gradebook has a **Load demo class** button: 24 made-up students, so you can try it before using your own file. To get your own file, find **Export** (or **Download**) in your gradebook and choose CSV.

**How it fits with your chat**

- **The Offline option** (the usual way), if your challenge is missing work: type the total missing and how many kids have 3 or more missing into your chat (or let Class Pulse count them: drop your gradebook in → Generate → Copy → paste) → your AI helps with the one thing you're trying → print the cards or messages in Class Tools.
- **The Offline option, any other challenge** (attendance, participation, transitions, reading): just type your weekly count into the chat on Monday. You can skip Class Pulse.
- **The Claude for Teachers option** (only if you use Claude for Teachers *and* your district has said it's okay): you can share student information in the chat directly. Class Tools is still the quickest way to print cards, groups, and badges.

Badges and Random Groups share a small **memory file** (`class-state.json`) that remembers awards and who's worked together. It has names in it, so keep it on your computer (Documents is fine), not in your classroom assistant folder.
