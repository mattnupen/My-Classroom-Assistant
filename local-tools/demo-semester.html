Demo Semester

One made-up class and its AI, Otter, through a whole semester. Read it before you start, to see how the weekly routine works.

Everything here is fictional — the teacher, students, chats, and numbers. It shows what a real semester looks like. This class uses the Offline option: Otter only ever sees class totals. The same 24 made-up students are behind every "Load demo class" button.

The class

Mr. Nupen's 8th-grade English

Periods 1 & 3, 24 students. Sweet but chaotic. The one challenge, all year: missing work snowballs by mid-quarter.

The AI

Otter (they/them)

Named by student vote, beating "Toast" by 4 ballots. Calm, and loud about comebacks. Target the class set: from about 5 students with 3+ missing to 3 or fewer by winter break.

The routine

Mondays, about 10 minutes

One move at a time. Monday: bring the weekly class summary and say "run Monday." About every two weeks, a check: keep, adjust, or switch. Mr. Nupen decides.

Did the class reach its target?

Yes. From 5, 6, 5 students with 3+ missing (Weeks 1–3) to 3 or fewer, held for four weeks. Low point: 9 in Week 5, when the essay outline landed.

What we kept

Reminders naming one task and one moment (Week 3, fixed in Week 5). A weekly note to every family (Week 7). The student self-check (Week 10): the number never showed a change from it; Mr. Nupen kept it because students used it.

What we learned

Vague reminders did nothing (Week 4). Changing one thing at a time kept every check readable.
