Class Pulse

Turns your gradebook into a weekly class summary with no names, for your AI. Quickest way: drop your gradebook in Step 1, click Generate, then Copy. The defaults are fine.

Names stay on this computer. The summary has class totals only — no names, IDs, individual grades, or anything that points to one student.

Step 1 — This week's gradebook

No file yet? Load demo class tries it with 24 made-up students. To get your own: in your gradebook, find Export (or Download) and choose CSV.

Optional settings — skip these unless the numbers look wrong

Columns

Names are used only to count students. They never appear in the output. Period is what we group the summary by.

Assignment columns (0 included) — adjust…

Where to draw the lines

Each student lands in one of three groups by how many assignments they're missing. The defaults work for most classes.

Compare to last week

With it, the summary shows what changed (e.g., "Strong: 6 → 8 (+2)"). Without it, you get this week's numbers only.

Step 2 — Make the summary

Send this week's summary to your AI

Click Copy on the summary just below, paste it into the chat, and say “here's this week's summary.” That's all.

Or save it to your classroom assistant folder instead — optional

A period, a course, a nickname — never a student's name.

  1. Click Downloaded
  2. Open your Downloads folder and find pulse-(today's date).md.
  3. Drag it into the pulses folder. It’s in your classroom assistant folder, inside my-classroom, next to local-tools. No my-classroom folder yet? Copy and paste instead.
  4. In the chat, say “run Monday.”

Either way, your AI sees only what's in the box below: class totals, no names.
