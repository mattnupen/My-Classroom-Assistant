# What's New

Plain-language notes on each version, for teachers. To get an update, say **"update my assistant"** in your chat; your AI walks you through it and never changes your class's notes.

## 2.1 — September 23, 2026

**In one sentence:** your assistant now talks like a colleague, sticks to one challenge all year, and makes slides you can open in PowerPoint or Google Slides.

- **A shorter first chat.** Five quick questions, one at a time, then a one-page plan for this week. Stop partway? Say "let's keep going" next time.
- **One challenge, one idea at a time.** It checks about every two weeks whether things moved, and you decide: keep, adjust, or switch.
- **It shows its homework.** Every idea says where it comes from and how strong the research is. We re-checked all the research and fixed some overstated claims.
- **Less work for you.** About 10 minutes a week, and at most one small thing to do outside class.
- **Plain names for how student names are handled.**
  - **The Offline option** (the default): names stay on your computer, and you just share class numbers.
  - **The Claude for Teachers option**: only if your district has said it's okay. Then you can share student information in the chat.
  - Either way, no student's name ends up on anything the class sees.
- **Slides for PowerPoint and Google Slides.** A clean, high-contrast design, with your script in the speaker notes.
- **A fresh look for Class Tools** (the page with your offline apps) that matches the website, with a "How Class Tools works" panel at the top.
- **A simpler app builder** with a step-by-step install guide.

**Anything to do?** If you added the app builder before, remove the old one in Claude (Customize → Skills) and upload the new `teacher-app-builder-skill-upload.zip`. On the Claude for Teachers option, check that your district's permission covers sharing in the chat.

## 2.0 — September 22, 2026

**In one sentence:** everything about your class now lives in one folder, `my-classroom`, that updates never touch, so copying it is a complete backup.

- **Five ready-made challenges** (missing work, attendance, participation, transitions, reading), each with its research and weekly moves.
- **A choice about student names.** Keeping names on your computer is the default; sharing them is only for districts that have approved it.
- **Your class page in the chat.** Say "show my class page" for your goal and numbers.
- **Apps you build are kept** safely with your class and show up in the Class Tools sidebar.

**Anything to do?** Say "update my assistant" and follow along (it asks you to copy your folder first). Then reinstall `teacher-app-builder-skill-upload.zip` in Claude under Customize → Skills.
