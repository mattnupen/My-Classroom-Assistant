# Weekly Rhythm

How a week works once your classroom AI is set up. The rule of thumb: **the smallest routine that keeps the challenge moving**. Everything past the minimum week is optional.

Students never use the AI, and you don't use it during class. It works through you, before and after.

---

## The minimum week: about 10 minutes

**1. Before Monday: get this week's number (a couple of minutes).** Which number depends on your challenge:
- **Missing work (from the gradebook).** Offline option: type two numbers from your gradebook, the total missing and how many kids have 3 or more missing. If counting is a pain, let **Class Pulse** count them: download your gradebook with its **Export** button, drag it into Class Pulse, click **Generate**, then **Copy**, and paste it into the chat. Claude for Teachers option: just share your gradebook in the chat.
- **Attendance, voices, reading, or a timed transition (a count you keep).** Have the number ready: this week's absence count, your clipboard tally of voices (two lessons a week), or each day's stopwatch time. You'll type it in on Monday.

**2. Monday: open a chat and say hi, or "what am I doing this week?" (about 5 minutes).** A count you gave on Friday counts; no need to send it again. Your AI records the number, updates your class page, and gives you the week on one page for the **one** thing you're trying: what to do each day, what to say, and anything to print. You look it over and change what you want.

**3. During the week: do the one thing you're trying.** That's the whole job.

**Every second Monday is a check** (a couple of minutes more). Your AI shows how the number has moved, names anything else that could explain it (a short week, testing, a flu wave), and suggests whether to keep it, adjust it, or switch to the next one. You decide. See `challenge-cycle.md`.

**Missed a week?** Nothing breaks. Your AI notes the gap and moves the next check back a week.

**Forgot to count?** On a day you'll need a count, your AI can give you a one-line sticky note for your clipboard ("Friday: count who spoke").

---

## The Later list (for the AI)

The class page keeps a short Later list: problems the teacher parked and name-free follow-ups (a crisis check, a counselor reply, a call home). You hold it so the teacher doesn't have to, and never mention it (or the class page) unless the teacher asks.
- A crisis follow-up comes first, at the start of the next conversation (`safety-rules.md` rule 1). Then at most one follow-up about people, following `how-we-talk.md` "Close the loop on people."
- A parked problem comes back when the teacher asks, or at the end of a season.

**Setup offers** aren't the teacher's chores, so they never go on the class page. Offer **one** at a time, at the end of a calm chat, never on a heavy day, a rushed morning, in a week they said they were swamped, or in a reply that already ends with a question: what it is in plain words, how long, and that it can wait. Note in `your-classroom-ai.md` ("Offered so far") what you offered and the answer. "Not today" means later: offer it once more on a calm day, then stop. **A "no" ends it:** don't offer that again, or anything that depends on it, unless the teacher brings it up. Exception: after a crisis flag, `safety-rules.md` rule 1 still applies, so offer the blank crisis card once more on a calm day.
- **Naming the AI:** only if the teacher chose "Later" in the first chat, once the first week has gone reasonably well: *"Want to give me a name? The class can vote, or you can pick. About 15 minutes whenever you're ready."* If a class vote is pending, ask once how it went instead (`first-chat.md`, "The class vote"). Launch talk (principal, letter home) comes only after a name, with: *"None of this is needed for what you're doing now."*
- **The crisis card**, in the first few weeks: *"Want a card for your desk with your counselor's number and 988, in case a student ever says something worrying? Five minutes to fill in by hand."*
- **The practice run** only when the teacher asks. Once they're using it for real, it isn't needed.

## What "run Monday" does (for the AI)

Follow the Monday ritual in `CLAUDE.md` and the rules in `challenge-cycle.md`:
1. Get the number: the newest weekly class summary, a pasted summary, or what the teacher types or shares. Save class totals only. A summary that starts "DEMO CLASS" (or a file named `pulse-DEMO-…`) is practice with made-up students: talk it through, but never write it to the class page, the class story, the trend table, or Class Tools' cards, and skip it when reading `my-classroom/pulses/`.
2. Add it to the class page (`dashboard.md`, and `dashboard-data.js` if a card changed).
3. Read the numbers like a coach. If it's a check week, run the check and recommend keep, adjust, or switch. Otherwise, **don't add a second classroom move.** Give the week on one page for the current one (`challenge-cycle.md` §1).
4. Every suggestion names its source in a few words and how strong the research is, in plain words.
5. Make what the teacher approves. Class materials go in `my-classroom/for-class/[date]/`; anything with a student's name is given in the chat, not saved, unless the teacher asks for a file (see the data policy). Then add a short line to `class-story.md` (a full entry on check weeks).

If there's no new number, say so and ask for it. Never guess.

## Optional, when you have time

- **A two-minute morning check-in.** Tell your AI what you're teaching today and anything you noticed yesterday; it drafts what you ask for. Helpful, never required.
- **Printing and prep, only when the current move calls for it:**
  - Missing-work cards for each student: the **Progress Cards** app.
  - Notes home from one template: the **Parent Messages** app.
  - Certificates for effort and comebacks: the **Badges** app.

  These apps run on your computer, and names stay there.

---

## Every 4–6 weeks: the season finale (15 minutes of prep, part of one class)

Your AI makes the scoreboard (class totals, week by week) and a three-question look-back for the class (a short anonymous survey). Students answer anonymously, and you put the answers through the **Feedback Cleaner** app before sharing them. Then you decide what to try next together. By default, the class **keeps the same challenge** and starts the next strategy. It only changes if you decide it should.

## Once a season, in the background

Your AI re-checks the research behind your challenge. It only mentions it if something changed.

## End of a quarter or semester (optional, about 20 minutes)

Read the class story back together with your AI. What moved? Which moves stuck? Is this still the right challenge? Should the AI's name or voice change? Your AI drafts a one-page reflection if you want one.

---

## When the AI's work starts to slip

If drafts get generic, off-tone, or stop sounding like your class:
1. **Say what's wrong.** "The last two notes home sounded corporate. You're supposed to sound [like this]." It will adjust.
2. **Sharpen the persona.** Ask your AI to show you its name-and-voice page, and tighten the voice together.
3. **Start a fresh chat.** A long chat full of one-off requests can blur things. Close it and open a new one in the same folder. Your AI picks up from its saved notes.
