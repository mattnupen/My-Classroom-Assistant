# The AI's Instructions

This folder is what your classroom AI reads at the start of every chat. It's the AI's job description: how to teach, how to talk to you, and the rules it never breaks. You don't have to read any of it — but you can, and you'll see exactly what it's been told.

**Your own stuff isn't here.** Your AI's name and voice, your class story, and your choice about student names and grades all live in the `my-classroom` folder. Updates replace this folder but never touch `my-classroom`.

## What's in here

| File | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `challenge-deck/` | Five ready-made challenges — missing work, attendance, participation, transitions, reading. Pick one and the research, the number to watch, and the ideas to try, in order, come with it. |
| `challenge-cycle.md` | How it works on your one challenge all year: one idea at a time, a check about every two weeks, and you decide keep, adjust, or switch. |
| `how-we-talk.md` | How the AI talks with you: plain, brief, one next step, no tech talk. |
| `safety-rules.md` | The rules it never breaks — reporting concerns to you, never naming students in front of the class, and more. You can add rules; you can't remove them. |
| `teaching-principles.md` | The research-backed habits it follows when it makes anything for your class. |
| `research-foundations.md` | The studies behind those habits, for when you want to know why. |
| `evidence-engine.md` | How it looks up research on *your* goal and writes down what it found. |
| `evidence-packs/` | The research it's already gathered for the five challenges. Research it gathers for your own goal is saved in `my-classroom`. |
| `persona-packs.md` | Three starter personas — ready to remix, or to put on a ballot for a student vote. |
| `weekly-rhythm.md` | The small weekly routine (about 10 minutes a week) and what 'run Monday' does. |

## If something feels off

The AI is usually doing what it was told. Tell it what's wrong in plain words — "you're too wordy," "that doesn't fit my kids," "our week doesn't work like that" — and it will fix the right file. If it ever asks for student names when you haven't chosen to share them, or does anything that feels unsafe, stop and tell it to re-read the safety rules.
