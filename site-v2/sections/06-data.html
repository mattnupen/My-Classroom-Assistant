Privacy

By default, the AI never sees a student’s name.

Your course export and your own notes can go straight to the AI. Anything with student names, like your gradebook or student feedback, goes through offline apps on your computer first. They turn it into totals, and only the totals reach the AI.

Diagram. Course data, meaning your LMS course export and your own notes, goes straight to your assistant. Student data, meaning your gradebook and student feedback, goes to offline apps on your laptop, which your assistant built. They send on only cleaned totals, such as 8 students behind on Unit 3. Optional: if you use Claude for Teachers and your district gives permission, you can choose to share student data with your assistant directly.

Whether you may upload identifiable student data is your district’s decision, not Anthropic’s. Ask first.
