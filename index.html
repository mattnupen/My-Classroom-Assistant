Hand one classroom problem to an AI, and it keeps working on it all year.

A semi-autonomous AI agent that takes on one challenge in your class, like missing work or attendance. Each week it checks what’s working, adjusts its strategy and sends you the slides and plans to run. You decide what reaches students.

Setup

Setup isjust a chat.

In about ten minutes it asks what you teach, what you want to change and what it should never do. Then it writes up your assistant’s goal and rules.

What we settled

  • Goal: less missing work
  • Name and voice: up to your class
  • House rule: no calling kids out

Research

Its choices come from research.

When you set a goal, it looks up what education research says works for it. It saves each finding on a short card with the source and how strong the evidence is, so you can check its work.

Weekly rhythm

Once a week, it makes a plan. You decide.

Each week you give it two things: class totals from your gradebook and anonymous student feedback. It checks whether last week’s strategy worked, keeps it or tries something new, and drafts what you need. Nothing reaches your students except through you.

Privacy

By default, the AI never sees a student’s name.

Your course export and your own notes can go straight to the AI. Anything with student names, like your gradebook or student feedback, goes through offline apps on your computer first. They turn it into totals, and only the totals reach the AI.

Diagram. Course data, meaning your LMS course export and your own notes, goes straight to your assistant. Student data, meaning your gradebook and student feedback, goes to offline apps on your laptop, which your assistant built. They send on only cleaned totals, such as 8 students behind on Unit 3. Optional: if you use Claude for Teachers and your district gives permission, you can choose to share student data with your assistant directly.

Tools

Need a tool nobody makes? It writes one.

Ask for a seating chart that keeps two kids apart, or a reading tracker for your room. It builds a working app, checks that it never sends data online, and adds it to your sidebar.

Each tool is one file you double-click.
